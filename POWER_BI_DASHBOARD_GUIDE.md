# Power BI Dashboard Guide

## Recommended KPI Cards
- Total Net Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

## Recommended Visuals
1. Line chart: Monthly Net Sales
2. Bar chart: Sales by Category
3. Bar chart: Sales by City
4. Bar chart: Top 10 Products
5. Donut chart: Category contribution
6. Table: City + Category + Net Sales

## Slicers
- Order Date
- City
- Category
- Product

## Suggested Dashboard Title
Sales Performance Dashboard

## Data Model
Use the `sales_data` table as the main fact table.
